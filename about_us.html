About Us

Welcome to Friendship On Wheels! We are dedicated to providing support and assistance to those who need it most. Our mission is to connect volunteers with individuals who require special care and attention, ensuring that everyone in our community feels valued and supported.

At Friendship On Wheels, we offer a range of services including meal delivery, transportation assistance, light housekeeping, and companionship. Our goal is to create a network of support that fosters compassion, empathy, and friendship among all members of our community.

For more information, feel free to explore our website or contact us directly. We're here to help!
