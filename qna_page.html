Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible for friendship on wheels?

We provide services to those who may be vulenerable and fragile, individuals needing special needs or any assistance to perform any day to day, simple tasks.

These individuals can include any elderly, disabled individuals or any individuals in requirements of special needs.


What services do we provide?

Friendship on Wheels (FOW) aims to connect requestors and volunteers to provide multiple services to requestors with help in need. Some of theese services include: Meal delivery, Transportation Services, Light housekeeping services and Companionship. Friendship on Wheels is not just a meal delivery service but a program to connect individuals of different community.


What is the food like? What is the price for the meals?

Friendship on Wheels provide a very large variety of meal options. We made sure our options are inclusive of all dietary requirements, including options of vegan and vegetarian meals and pescatarian options. Under all meal options will be allergen warnings for those with special dietary requirements. We also provide alternative options for gluten free diets and high protein/fibre/vitamin needs. For further enquiries on more personalised options, please contact us at your local branch!

What does volunteering involve?

To cater and satisfy all needs of both our volunteers and requestors, we interview, survey and categorise each volunteers for jobs they're most suited for. Depending on the needs of each volunteer, we will help assign and schedule appropriate work that will best fit the volunteer.


How long would the volunteering hours be in a week?

Friendship on Wheels support each every users' lifestyle and preferences. To best suit everybodys needs, we provide a flexible scheduling system for all users to enter their availabilities and we'll try our best to organise volunteer hours. That is, each volunteer is able to choose the timeframe, the date and the duration for the volunteer hours!
