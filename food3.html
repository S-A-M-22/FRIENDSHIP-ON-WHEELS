Burrata with Stone Fruit Salad

Meal Description

This is a light and refreshing dish that features creamy burrata cheese surrounded by a salad of seasonal stone fruits.

Ingredients: Burrata Cheese (Pasteurized Milk, Cream, Calf Rennet, Salt), Peaches, Nectarines, Olive Oil, Lemon Juice, Honey, Mint Leaves.

!Allergen Warning!

This dish contains dairy (milk).
