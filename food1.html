Meal Information

Buffalo Chicken Wings with Celery Sticks and Blue Cheese Dressing

Meal Description

This dish is a variation of classic Buffalo wings, which are chicken wings that have been battered or breaded and then baked or fried. They are then coated in a spicy Buffalo sauce, which is typically made with hot sauce, butter, and vinegar. The wings in the image are served with celery sticks and blue cheese dressing, which are traditional accompaniments for Buffalo wings.

Ingredients: Chicken Wings, Vegetable Oil, Unbleached Wheat Flour, Baking Powder, Paprika, Cayenne Pepper, Garlic Powder, Onion Powder, Salt, Black Pepper. Sauce: Hot Sauce (Distilled Vinegar, Aged Cayenne Red Peppers, Salt, Garlic Powder, Natural Flavors), Butter, Honey. Dip: Blue Cheese Dressing (Cultured Pasteurized Milk, Buttermilk, Vinegar, Blue Cheese Crumbles, Canola Oil, Salt, Garlic Powder, Onion Powder, Spices). Contains: Wheat, Milk, Soy (in Blue Cheese Dressing).

!Allergen Warning!

This dish contains dairy (milk and cheese), wheat (in the batter) and chicken.
