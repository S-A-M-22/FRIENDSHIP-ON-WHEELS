Our Services

Comprehensive Meal Delivery Solutions

Our primary service is the deliver to you nutritious meals directly to at your home. You can specify your meal preferences including time, quantity, temperature (hot or cold), and dietary restrictions. We are tailored to meet your specific health, cultural, and personal tastes.

Friendly Visits

Feeling lonely? Our service provides friendly visits. These interactions help combat loneliness and ensure that you remain connected to your community.

Safety Checks

During each visit, our volunteers perform quick safety checks to ensure youe well-being, offering you with peace of mind.

Customisable Volunteer Matching

We schedule and match you to volunteers based on your language spoken, mobility needs, and personal preferences. Our system also adheres to our policy of refreshing volunteer-requestor pairs annually, ensuring new interactions and ongoing support.

Additional Services are provided upon request once scheduled.

Requestable Services:

  • Driving services
  • Assistance in Housekeeping
