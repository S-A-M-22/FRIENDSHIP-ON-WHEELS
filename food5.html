Meal Information

Laksa: Spicy Noodle Soup

Meal Description

Laksa is a vibrant and flavorful noodle soup originating in Southeast Asia. This fragrant dish features rice noodles in a rich and creamy coconut milk broth, packed with seafood, vegetables, and aromatic spices. Its complex flavors range from spicy and savory to slightly sweet and tangy.

Ingredients: Spicy Noodle Soup (Coconut Milk, Water, Rice Vermicelli Noodles, Fish Balls (Fish Surimi Paste, Starch, Sugar, Salt, Spices), Shrimp (cooked, deveined), Tofu Puffs, Bean Sprouts, Vegetable Oil, Curry Paste (Chillies, Lemongrass, Galangal, Turmeric, Candlenuts, Shallots, Shrimp Paste, Salt), Fish Sauce (Anchovies, Salt, Sugar), Palm Sugar, Lime Juice).

!Allergen Warning!

This dish contains Fish, Shellfish (shrimp), Soy (in fish balls and curry paste).
