Roasted Potato with Vegetables

Meal Description

This is a side dish featuring roasted vegetables. Roasting vegetables concentrates their flavors and brings out their natural sweetness.

Ingredients: Potatoes, Bell Peppers (Red, Yellow, Orange), Red Onion, Zucchini, Cherry Tomatoes, Olive Oil, Sea Salt, Black Pepper.

!Allergen Warning!

This dish is free of the major allergens unless otherwise noted.
