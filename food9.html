Chopped Asparagus Salad with Goat Cheese and Hazelnuts

Meal Description

Treat yourself to a light and refreshing salad that's bursting with flavor and texture. Tender asparagus spears are chopped and tossed with crisp romaine lettuce for a delightful base. Creamy goat cheese crumbles offer a tangy counterpoint to the sweetness of the asparagus, while crunchy hazelnuts add a satisfying nuttiness. A simple dressing of olive oil and lemon juice ties all the elements together, and a sprinkle of salt and pepper enhances the natural flavors. This salad is a perfect light lunch or a delightful starter for a summer meal.

Ingredients: Asparagus, Lettuce (romaine), Hazelnuts, Goat Cheese, Olive Oil, Lemon Juice, Salt, Pepper.

!Allergen Warning!

This dish contains dairy (goat cheese) and tree nuts (hazelnuts).
