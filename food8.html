Meal Information

Ramen

Meal Description

Embrace the comfort and warmth of a steaming bowl of Ramen! This iconic Japanese noodle soup features springy wheat noodles bathed in a rich and flavorful broth. The broth can be based on chicken, pork, or even vegetables, each offering a unique taste profile.

Ingredients: Noodles, Broth (Chicken or Vegetable), Meat (Chicken, Pork or Beef - optional), Vegetables (Bok Choy, Scallions, Nori, Bamboo Shoots, Egg - optional), Seasoning (Miso Paste, Soy Sauce).

!Allergen Warning!

This dish contains wheat (in the noodles), soy (in the miso paste and soy sauce), and egg.
