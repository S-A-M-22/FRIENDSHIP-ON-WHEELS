INSIGHTS

We are a non-profit initiative dedicated to providing support to the elderly and people with disabilities. Our goal is to not only deliver meals to their homes, but also offer additional services such as friendly visits and safety checks by our carefully vetted volunteers, all from the comfort of their own homes. By simply registering, they can access everything they need with just a few clicks.
