Meal Information

Black Paella

Meal Description

Dive into a taste of the Spanish coast with Black Paella! This dramatic dish features bomba rice cooked in a flavorful broth infused with squid ink, lending it a unique jet-black color. Tender pieces of chicken, squid, and mussels mingle with the rice, while vibrant bell peppers and tomatoes add pops of color and flavor. A sprinkle of paprika adds a touch of smokiness, and precious saffron threads lend a subtle floral aroma and beautiful yellow hue (although the squid ink might obscure it a bit!). Finished with a squeeze of fresh lemon, Black Paella is a feast for the senses and a true culinary adventure.

Ingredients: Bomba Rice, Water, Chicken, Squid, Mussels, Garlic, Onion, Bell Pepper, Tomato, Paprika, Saffron, Lemon.

!Allergen Warning!

This dish contains shellfish (mussels), fish and chicken.
